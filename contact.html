About Geneva

With 20 years of rabbit breeding experience, I'm a dedicated ARBA (American Rabbit Breeders Association) member focused on raising healthy, friendly Champagne d'Argent and Silver Fox rabbits in Alaska.

My textile art journey began when I taught myself to knit, developing original designs inspired by Alaska's incredible wildlife and landscapes. Each piece is mathematically patterned and handcrafted with natural fibers.

Credentials & Expertise

• ARBA Member with 20 years rabbit breeding experience

• Self-taught fiber artist specializing in original designs

• Award-winning textile artist (Southeast Alaska State Fair)

• Traditional fur tanning services for rabbit and snowshoe hare

• Custom commissions for wildlife, fantasy, and wearable art
