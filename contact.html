The Story of Geneva's Trading Post

I grew up between Sitka, Alaska and the San Juan Islands, in 4-H from age 10 for astronomy and textile art. Started with meat rabbits at 17, earned dual degrees in physics/math from Evergreen (NSF-funded), then an MS in Physics from UAF studying auroras. Bought my Fairbanks house during grad school—homesteading became my anchor. Married my husband (a Glacier Bay park ranger and now business co-owner) in 2019. When COVID hit, my off-grid skills carried me through seven layoffs in twelve months. In 2021, we bought our off-grid Gustavus homestead near Glacier Bay, while keeping our original on-grid Fairbanks home. I'm now a Certified Master Gardener (2024), active ARBA member, former 4-H Leader (2009-2013), and retraining through UAF. Still an amateur astronomer after all these years.
