Meat Rabbit Husbandry & Cage Building FREE

📅 June 20, 2026 · 1:30–4:30 PM · Gustavus Library Conference Room

Join us for a hands-on workshop! We'll have two litters of nine-week-old babies plus nest box babies to demonstrate proper handling and growth stages. Learn how to construct a large grow-out cage for fryers, perform a basic health check on rabbits, and identify good commercial body type.
