Our Breeding Program

We focus on two heritage breeds perfect for Alaska's climate:

  • Champagne d'Argent: Known for their beautiful silvered fur that develops with age and excellent meat production
  • Silver Fox: A dual-purpose breed with remarkable fur quality and good size
  • Goals: Produce healthy, friendly rabbits with excellent meat conformation and quality pelts
  • Care: All rabbits receive individual attention, proper nutrition, and clean, spacious living conditions
  • Location: Raised in Fairbanks, Alaska - acclimated to cold weather conditions

Rabbit Fur Services

I hand-tan rabbit and snowshoe hare skins for $5-10 per skin, depending on pelt size. This traditional skill allows us to use the entire animal and create beautiful, warm fur items. (Photos of rabbit skin hats and products coming soon!)
