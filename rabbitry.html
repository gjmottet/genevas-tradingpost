Last year's Harvest Festival - Lucille and her litter with grass local kids collected. Free petting zoo to socialize our bunnies (and spread the rabbit love!).

🐇 What to expect: Friendly bunnies of all ages. Kids and adults welcome to pet, hold (with guidance), and learn about rabbit care. Our rabbits are well-socialized and love attention!

📍 Find us: Look for the bunny fence at Sunday Market (A&E Trading Company, noon-4pm, weather permitting) and at special events like the Gustavus Strawberry Festival.
