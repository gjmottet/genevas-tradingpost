Our Alaskan Rabbit Family

Heritage rabbits raised with care in Fairbanks and Gustavus for meat and fur. Friendly, healthy stock for homesteaders.

🐰 Meet the Bunnies

Champagne d'Argent and Silver Fox rabbits with beautiful fur, excellent meat conformation, and friendly dispositions. Each gets individual attention in our Alaska setup.

🧬 Breeding Goals: I'm focusing on Champagne d'Argent — a historic French meat & fur breed, often called the "Wagyu of rabbit breeds" for its rich flavor and fine bone structure. My stock has excellent silvering but runs a bit small, so I'm breeding to increase size while preserving that gorgeous coat and commercial body type.
