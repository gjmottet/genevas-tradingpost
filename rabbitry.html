Rabbit & Snowshoe Hare Fur Tanning

Traditional hand-tanning for beautiful, usable fur

Hand-Tanned Rabbit & Snowshoe Hare Pelts

I offer traditional hand-tanning services for rabbit and snowshoe hare pelts. Using time-honored methods, I transform raw hides into soft, usable fur perfect for crafts, clothing, and traditional Alaskan items.

This service honors the full use of the animal, creating beautiful, warm fur items while practicing sustainable harvest traditions. Perfect for hunters, homesteaders, and crafters who want to utilize every part of their harvest.

$5–10 per skin (based on pelt size)
