Hand Tanning Services

I offer hand tanning for domestic rabbit, snowshoe hare, and squirrel hides using the traditional salt/alum method. I tan in small batches of 10–12 pelts, the same way I tan my own.

Hide TypeFresh or Properly FrozenProblem Pelts*
Baby rabbit, squirrel, snowshoe hare$6$20
Adult prime rabbit$12$20

*Problem pelts include freezer-burned, dried out, or any hide requiring significant extra fleshing time.

Batch Options

Add to my next batch – I add your pelts to my own and tan when I have 10–12 total ready. Turnaround: 3–6 weeks.

On-demand batch – You provide 10–12 pelts and I run a dedicated batch. Turnaround: 3 weeks from receipt.

Tanning Policies

Before I start: I'll assess your pelts and let you know if any fall into the "problem pelt" category. You can decide whether to proceed or opt out.

If something goes wrong:

  • Good condition pelts (fresh or properly frozen): If I damage a pelt during tanning (tear, slip, etc.), I'll offer a 50% refund on that hide.
  • Problem pelts: Because these carry higher risk from the start, I cannot offer refunds if they fail during tanning. You'll know their condition before we begin.

I'll communicate along the way if anything unexpected comes up. My goal is to return the best possible pelts to you.
